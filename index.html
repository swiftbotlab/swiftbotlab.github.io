What I build

Small, dependable tools that take a repetitive job off your hands.

  • Order and lead-capture bots

    A bot that asks the right questions, collects orders or leads, and sends each one to you, a group or a Google Sheet.

  • Paid-access channels

    Charge for a private channel or group with Telegram Stars or crypto. Members are let in after payment.

  • Scheduled channel posting

    Queue posts in a CSV or Sheet and the bot publishes them on time, with photos and buttons.

  • Alerts from a Sheet or site

    Get a Telegram message when a price drops, an item is back in stock, a row is added or a page changes.

  • Scrapers into CSV or Sheets

    Listings, prices or catalogue data from public sites, cleaned and de-duplicated, on a schedule if you need it.

  • File, CSV and Excel cleanup

    Merge, split, de-duplicate and reformat messy spreadsheets or batches of files with one repeatable script.

  • Browser automation for your own accounts

    Scripts that sign in as you and do the repetitive clicks: exports, uploads, form filling, reports.

  • Something else?

    If it is a bot, a script or a data job, describe it and I will tell you straight whether I can do it.

How it works

You see it working before any money changes hands.

  1. Describe the job

    Open the quote bot and answer 4 short questions: what it should do, when you need it, your budget, any extras.

  2. Fixed quote and date

    You get one price and a delivery date. Nothing starts until you agree.

  3. Try a working demo

    I build it and you test the real thing, usually within about 48 hours.

  4. Pay

    Only once it does what you asked. Crypto (the address comes with your quote) or Telegram Stars.

  5. Get the source

    Full source code and setup steps, so you own it and can run it anywhere.
